5 · Wipe — options & reserve (step 1 of 5)

Wipe options

A single zero pass is the sensible default. More passes add time and disk wear with no guarantee on SSD/Fusion.

Single pass — zeros (0x00)
Fast, low wear. Fills free space once with zeros. (Default)
Single pass — pseudorandom
Avoids any chance the filesystem skips writing zero blocks.
Three passes (advanced)
Zeros, then ones, then random. More time & wear, no extra guarantee.
Keep this much free (safety reserve): GiB

Recommended 43 GiB. Never below 10 GiB. The app re-checks space before every chunk and never fills the disk.

7 · PhotoRec — Audit (tab ②)

PhotoRec — Audit what's recoverable

Runs the bundled PhotoRec (the standard open-source recovery engine) READ-ONLY against a disk, and reconstructs any files it can find into a destination you choose — exactly what someone attempting recovery would get. It reports types and counts; it never modifies the source disk.

Honest limits: this reads host-accessible space only. SSD/Fusion controllers keep hidden cells no software can read, so a clean result is NOT proof nothing is recoverable. A whole-disk scan can take a long time and will ask for your administrator password.
